Projects

Braid visualizer

Basic usage: Connect pegs from top down as is convention. Each set of three pegs denotes a single braid. Multi-levelled for visualization of braid concatenation. To delete a strand, simply redrag the line. Crossings are set to "over" by default, so if you want P1→P2 to be above P2→P1, draw P1→P2 first. Enables saving images of canvas state and compression for desired presentation. Probably breakable.

Pi normality maze

Watch the digits of pi attempt to traverse a randomly generated maze. This was motivated by the conjectured normality of pi and the Twitch stream Pi Plays Pokemon by WinningSequence. Someday I may consider increasing the sequence limit but for now it is just hard-coded into the application.
